Heritage & Lineage

India’s Culinary Soul, Served in Daegu.

Varanasi is a tribute to the timeless culinary traditions of Northern India. Opened in 2024 near Samdeok Rodeo Alley by Chef Dhyan, our second-floor restaurant brings the aromas of Delhi straight to downtown Daegu.

Step through our door on Dongseong-ro 5-gil and the street noise fades into warm hospitality: live clay tandoor cooking, freshly crushed cloves and cardamom, and slow-brewed spiced masala chai.
