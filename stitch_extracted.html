Heritage & Lineage

India’s Culinary Soul, Served in Daegu.

Varanasi isn’t just a dining name; it is an homage to the sacred, timeless culinary traditions of Northern India. Opened in 2024 near Samdeok Rodeo Alley by Chef Dhyan, this second-floor sanctum bridges the spirited alleys of Delhi with downtown Daegu.

Step through our doorway on Dongseong-ro 5-gil and the bustling street noise transforms into an intoxicating sanctuary: the warm crackle of our imported clay tandoor, the scent of whole cloves and cardamom pods being crushed, and soothing Bollywood melodies in the background. We never adapt recipes to fit fast-food shortcuts — every pot is tended with ancestral patience.
